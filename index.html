📜 Checkers Rules & Modes

🎯 How to Play

  • Click a piece to select it, then click a highlighted destination cell to move.
  • Standard Move: Advance diagonally 1 square forward. (Kings can move in any diagonal direction).
  • Capture (Jump): Leap over an adjacent opponent's piece into an empty square beyond it.
  • King Me: Reach the furthest opposite rank to become a King ♔ with backwards movement ability.
  • Winning: Capture all opponent pieces or trap them so they have no legal moves.

⚡ Gameplay Modes

1. Feed-First Mode (Mandatory Capture)

If any capture/jump is available anywhere on the board for the active player, a capture MUST be executed. Continuous multi-jumps are mandatory.

2. Strategic Mode (Optional Initial Capture)

Players may choose standard non-capturing moves even if a capture is available. However, if a capture move is initiated, the continuous multi-jump rule still MANDATORILY applies for that piece.
