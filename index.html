📜 Checkers Rules & Modes

♟️ Player Position & Colors

  • Player 1 (You): Always placed at the bottom half of the board and moves UPWARDS to become Kings.
  • Player 2 / Computer: Placed at the top half of the board and moves DOWNWARDS.
  • Color Choice: Select your preferred color within any pair (e.g. Red or White).

🤖 Computer AI

  • Easy: Random valid moves and captures.
  • Hard: Minimax AI with Alpha-Beta pruning evaluating piece counts, position control, and king promotion.

⚡ Gameplay Modes

1. Feed-First Mode (Mandatory Capture)

If any capture/jump is available anywhere on the board for the active player, a capture MUST be executed. Continuous multi-jumps are mandatory.

2. Strategic Mode (Optional Initial Capture)

Players may choose standard non-capturing moves even if a capture is available. However, if a capture move is initiated, continuous multi-jumps MANDATORILY apply.
